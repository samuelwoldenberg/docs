# Light Metal Buildings / Carports Industry Overview

The **light metal building and carport industry** produces lightweight steel structures including:

- Carports
- Metal garages
- RV covers
- Utility buildings
- Barns and ag sheds
- Small commercial canopies

These products rely heavily on **pre-galvanized tubing, galvanized coil, pre-painted coil (PPGI), hat channel, and light-gauge purlins**. This guide is designed to help Titeg AEs, PMs, and ops teams understand the material needs, specifications, consumption patterns, and supply chain of this category.

---

# What These Manufacturers Buy

## Tubing (Structural Frame)

The structural frame of nearly all light metal buildings is built from standardized galvanized tubing.

### Most Common Size

**2.5" x 2.5" x 14ga, G60 or G90, non-spangle**

This is the _industry standard_ for posts, rafters, bows, and truss components.

### Additional Sizes

- 2" x 3" x 14ga
- 2.25" x 2.25" x 14ga
- 2" x 2" x 14ga (less common now)
- 2" x 4" x 14ga for larger buildings

### Tubing Preferences

- **G60** for most regions
- **G90** for coastal or high-humidity environments
- **Non-spangle** preferred for uniform appearance and weld quality
- Multiple lengths depending on building type:
  - 14'
  - 21'
  - 26'
  - 30'
  - 35'
  - 40'

Mills typically supply tubing either **cut-to-length** or as **40–48' master lengths** that manufacturers cut in-house.

# Flat Rolled Products

Flat-rolled galvanized coil is used for two main applications:

1. **Roll-forming roof and wall panels**
2. **Slitting and forming tubing (for larger operations)**

## Galvanized Coil (Unpainted)

Used for:

- Tube forming
- Structural components
- Flashing and trims (raw mill finish)

### Common Specs

- **14ga, 16ga, 18ga** for tube forming
- **26ga** for roof/wall panels
- **G60 or G90**
- **Non-spangle** preferred
- Coil widths:
  - 7"
  - 8.5"
  - 10"
  - 12"
  - 36"
  - 40.875"
  - 48"

---

# Pre-Painted Coil (PPGI)

PPGI (Pre-Painted Galvanized Iron) coil is used to produce all visible panels and trims of light metal buildings.

### Typical PPGI Specs

- **26ga** SMP (Silicon Modified Polyester) — industry standard
- Galvanized base: G40, G60, or G90
- Common applications:
  - Roof panels
  - Side panels
  - J-channel
  - Gable and corner trim
  - Base rail caps
  - Ridge caps

### Common Panel Profiles

- R-panel
- Ag-panel
- Corrugated ⅞"
- Custom roll-form shapes depending on region

Color consistency and lot matching are major pain points — especially for replacements.

# Secondary Components

## Hat Channel

Used for:

- Roof reinforcement
- Panel span support
- Girt or purlin alternatives
- Light framing in garages and barns

Specs:

- **26ga–28ga**
- Galvanized or painted
- Typically roll-formed in-house

---

## Light-Gauge Purlins

Used in:

- Larger garages
- Barn structures
- A-frame style buildings
- Commercial grade metal buildings

Specs:

- **C-purlin or Z-purlin**
- **16ga–12ga**
- Galvanized
- Cut-to-length depending on building width

# Manufacturing Workflow Overview

1. **Receive tubing and coil**
2. **Cut tubing** to predefined building dimensions
3. **Roll-form panels** from PPGI coil
4. **Roll-form trims** and flashing
5. **Bend hat channels** or light purlins
6. **Assemble building kits**
7. **Load trucks for installers**
8. **On-site installation** at end customer’s property

AEs should understand each step — it reveals key operational pain points.

# Core Pain Points Titeg Can Solve

### ⚠ Material Shortages

Missed deliveries create installation backlogs.

### ⚠ Price Volatility

Galvanized indices and paint cost swings impact profitability.

### ⚠ Quality Consistency

Non-spangle consistency, coating uniformity, and weldability are recurring issues.

### ⚠ Lead Time Variability

Coil and tube lead times can bottleneck production.

### ⚠ Color Matching

PPGI paint lots must match perfectly across multiple loads.

### ⚠ Freight Coordination

Many manufacturers rely on regional trucking and mixed inbound loads.

These are leverage points where Titeg creates value.

# What AEs Should Ask During a Visit

## Tubing Questions

- What are your most common tubing sizes?
- G60 or G90?
- Do you require non-spangle?
- Do you cut tubing in-house or buy cut-to-length?
- Weekly tubing consumption in truckloads?

## Coil / PPGI Questions

- Do you roll-form your own panels?
- Which colors do you run most?
- Coil widths and gauge requirements?
- How many coils per week?
- Current issues with supply?

## Operational Questions

- How many inbound trucks per week?
- Do you accept combined coil + tube loads?
- Any forklift restrictions or appointment requirements?

## Supplier Questions

- Top current suppliers?
- What do they do well?
- What problems do they create for you?
